# 🧪 Web3 Wallet & Testnet ETH Assignment (Sepolia)

## 🎯 Objective
Create a Web3 wallet, connect to Sepolia Testnet, acquire test ETH, and successfully send a transaction.

---

## ✅ Assignment Tasks

### **1. Create a Web3 Wallet**
- Install MetaMask: https://metamask.io/
- Create a new wallet.
- Securely store your recovery phrase offline.

---

### **2. Enable Sepolia Test Network**
- Open MetaMask → Settings → Advanced.
- Enable **Show Test Networks**.
- Switch network to **Sepolia Testnet**.

---

### **3. Get Testnet ETH (Sepolia)**
Use **any one** of the following faucets to claim test ETH:

- QuickNode Sepolia Faucet: https://faucet.quicknode.com/ethereum/sepolia  
- Infura Sepolia Faucet: https://www.infura.io/faucet/sepolia  
- Alchemy Sepolia Faucet: https://www.alchemy.com/faucets/ethereum-sepolia  
- Chainlink Faucets: https://faucets.chain.link/

**Requirement:**  
Receive at least **0.1 SepoliaETH** in your wallet.

---

### **4. Verify Balance on Explorer**
- Visit Sepolia Etherscan: https://sepolia.etherscan.io/
- Paste your wallet address.
- Confirm the test ETH is visible on the blockchain.

---
